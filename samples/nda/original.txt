Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its breach of this Agreement.

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.

8. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

9. Notices
Notices shall be sent by certified mail to the addresses on the signature page.

This Agreement, including Section 9, is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes prior discussions about the same subject.
