1. Purpose
The parties wish to evaluate a possible business relationship and may share non-public information for that purpose only.

2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public business, technical, and financial information disclosed by either party, including without limitation notes and derivatives, excluding information that is or becomes public other than by breach of this Agreement.

3. Obligations
The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information using reasonable care and shall use it only for the Purpose. Upon request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information.

4. Notices
Notices shall be sent by overnight courier to the addresses on the signature page.

5. Term
This Agreement remains in effect for one (1) year from the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the then-current term ends.

6. Indemnity
The Receiving Party shall indemnify the Disclosing Party against any and all claims arising from its breach of this Agreement, and its liability shall be unlimited for consequential damages.

7. Assignment
The Disclosing Party may assign this Agreement without the consent of the Receiving Party, including to an affiliate.

8. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, and each party waives its right to a jury trial.

9. Entire Agreement
This Agreement, including Section 9, is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes prior discussions about the same subject.
