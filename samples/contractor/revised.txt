1. Services
The Contractor will prepare the drawings described in the statement of work.

2. Payment
The Client shall pay a fixed fee of £11,000, plus VAT, half on signature and half on delivery.

3. Term
This Agreement ends one (1) month after the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for successive one (1) month terms until Client gives notice.

4. Intellectual Property
The Contractor owns the deliverables and grants the Client a non-exclusive licence to use them for the project named in the statement of work.

5. Non-Solicit
The Client shall not solicit the Contractor's employees during the term and for one (1) year after it ends.

6. Limitation of Liability
Each party's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid under this Agreement. A claim must be brought within twelve (12) months of the cause of action accruing.

7. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by Scots law. The parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Scotland.

8. Assignment
The Contractor may assign this Agreement without the consent of the Client.
